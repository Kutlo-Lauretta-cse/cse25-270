ABOUT PULA SCHOLARS

Bridging the gap between Batswana students and global opportunities

OUR MISSION

To promote education and academic development among young people and empower students to achieve their academic and career goals.

OUR VISION

To be the leading digital gateway for every student in Botswana seeking academic excellence, both locally and internationally.

Why We Built This

  • Accessibility: Simplified application processes for all skill levels.
  • Empowerment: Providing the tools for students to take charge of their future.
  • Local Focus: Specifically tailored for the Botswana educational landscape (DTEF).
