DTEF Local Sponsorship

Department of Tertiary Education Financing (Botswana)

Government funding for students admitted to local public and private institutions.

Application Requirements

  • Identification: Valid Omang (National Identity Card).
  • Admission: Official admission letter from a recognized local institution.
  • Results: Certified copy of BGCSE or equivalent results.
  • Age: Must be 35 years or younger at the time of application.
