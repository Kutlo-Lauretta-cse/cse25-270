Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I start an application?

First, create an account on our Sign Up page. Once logged in, go to the Scholarships section and choose between Local (DTEF) or International (UK) paths.


2. What is an 'Omang' and why is it needed?

An Omang is your Botswana National Identity Card. It is required for all local DTEF scholarship applications to prove citizenship.


3. I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click on the 'Login' link and select 'Forgot Password' to receive a reset link via your registered email address.
